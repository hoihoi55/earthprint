Earthprint

Privacy policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

What this is about

"Earthprint Autopilot" is a private tool used only by the owner of the Earthprint YouTube channel to manage that one channel. It is not offered to other users.

Data accessed

With the channel owner's permission, the tool reads comments on Earthprint videos, posts replies as the Earthprint channel, holds spam for review, and reads aggregate channel statistics (views, watch time, subscribers). It uses the YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API.

Use and sharing

Comment text may be processed by an AI model to draft a reply. Data is not sold, not used for advertising and not shared with third parties beyond the processing needed to draft replies. Logs are kept on the owner's own computer and deleted after 90 days.

Your choices

Viewers can delete their own comments on YouTube at any time. The channel owner can revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. YouTube's own policies apply: YouTube Terms of Service, Google Privacy Policy.
